## TJP archive and emblem

These files were downloaded from TJP’s existing public website. The source did not
supply individual photographer credits or a Creative Commons reuse license.
Copyright remains with the original rights holders; these assets are not relicensed.
JPEG assets were converted to WebP at quality 88, with a maximum width of 1920px
without upscaling. The emblem is a PNG. Responsive crops use CSS object-position;
lightbox views retain the complete images. The favicon is a 64px copy of the emblem.

## Editorial context

Founder and chairman profiles summarise TJP’s published leadership pages. The
Hamid Gul archive portrait is also listed as media item 9497 in the original
[WordPress media record](https://tjp.org.pk/wp-json/wp/v2/media/9497). Its capture
date and photographer are not specified. The founder banner is an illustrative
montage from the old homepage, not documentation of completed TJP projects.

Meeting captions distinguish Chairman Abdullah Hamid Gul from founder Gen. Hamid
Gul. No unverified photograph of a founder meeting has been added. Dates on
archive articles refer to the original publication, confirmed using the source
[WordPress post records](https://tjp.org.pk/wp-json/wp/v2/posts?per_page=100).
The book invitation is archived, not an upcoming event.

Three retained sample editorial articles are labelled in both languages; they
do not claim to report real party events. No live news feed or CMS is introduced.
